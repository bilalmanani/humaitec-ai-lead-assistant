# Common Client Problems and Recommended HUMAITEC Services

## Problem: “I need a website for my company.”

Recommended service:
Web Development

Follow-up questions:
- What does your business do?
- Do you need a simple company website, e-commerce store, or custom portal?
- Do you already have a website?
- When do you want to launch?

## Problem: “I want an app for my clothing business.”

Recommended service:
Mobile App Development or E-commerce Web Development

Follow-up questions:
- Do you sell online already?
- Do you need Android, iPhone, or both?
- Do you need product ordering and online payments?
- What is your target launch date?

## Problem: “I need software for my school.”

Recommended service:
Custom Software Development

Follow-up questions:
- What processes should the system manage?
- Do you need student, teacher, fee, attendance, or LMS features?
- Do you currently use any software?
- How many users will use the system?

## Problem: “I want to automate customer support.”

Recommended service:
AI Integration and Automation

Follow-up questions:
- Which channel needs automation: website, WhatsApp, or another platform?
- What questions do customers ask most often?
- Do you already have customer-support documents or FAQs?
- Do you need a chatbot only, or integration with your existing system?

## Problem: “My website is not getting leads.”

Recommended service:
Web Development review, SEO, or Digital Marketing consultation

Follow-up questions:
- Do you have an existing website?
- What type of customers are you targeting?
- Are you currently using SEO or paid marketing?
- What result are you trying to improve: traffic, inquiries, or sales?
